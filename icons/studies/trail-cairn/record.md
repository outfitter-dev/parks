# Trail cairn · Linear · study

**Brief.** Mark a point on a hiking route where a stack of stones helps someone find the trail. Closest reference considered was Solar Signpost. Its arrows communicate direction and its upright post implies a built sign. The cairn needs to read as a marker even without an arrow.

**Source and method.** Original hand-authored SVG for this workflow trial. Solar Signpost by 480 Design supplied a weight and detail reference. No source paths imported, no AI generation run, no product usage claimed. [Solar Signpost source](https://github.com/480-Design/Solar-Icon-Set/blob/main/icons/SVG/Linear/Map%20%26%20Location/Signpost.svg)

**Decision 1.** Use three uneven, progressively wider stones. The offset contours are intended to distinguish the mark from a database or layer stack. Omit ground and landscape details to preserve the gaps at toolbar size.

**Quiver brief for a comparison pass.** “Draw a trail cairn for a hiking map. Three low irregular stones, largest at the base, each with a distinct silhouette. Use the supplied Solar Signpost image for line weight and optical scale. Center the stack in a 24 × 24 frame, use editable 1.5 px monochrome strokes and open space between the stones. No text, arrow, shadow or scenery.” Save only candidates that change a design decision. Record the actual prompt, inputs, model shown, edits and selection rationale when this pass runs.
